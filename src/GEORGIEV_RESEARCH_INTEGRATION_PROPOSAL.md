## Detailed Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Zanzibar Red Colobus Project Integration (Immediate)

**Goal:** Centralise Zanzibar field data and automate compliance reporting

**Specific Features Georgiev Lab Would Use:**

1. **Observation Logging**
   - Field team enters: date, location (GPS), species, behaviour codes, observer, field notes
   - On-field tablet app with offline capability
   - Automatic species ID with AI confidence scoring
   - Physiological samples logged (hormone levels, immunological markers)

2. **Compliance Dashboard**
   - Real-time IUCN Endangered status tracking
   - Vehicle collision hotspot monitoring (geo-spatial flagging)
   - Population trend tracking (increasing/decreasing/stable)
   - Habitat quality assessments
   - Missing data alerts (surveys overdue, incomplete fields)

3. **Field Report Automation**
   - Auto-generates structured field reports from observation logs
   - Sections: Survey methodology, species recorded, conservation status, threats, recommendations
   - PDF export for grant reports and conservation authority submissions
   - Natural England UK BAP reporting compatibility

4. **GIS Integration**
   - Map observation locations and collision incidents
   - Overlay with habitat suitability predictions
   - Visualise conservation priority areas

5. **Data Quality & Audit Trail**
   - Every record timestamped, observer-attributed
   - Version history of species assessments
   - Verification workflows for submitted data

**Outcome:**
- Zanzibar Red Colobus data becomes centrally managed, IUCN-compliant, and instantly reportable
- Field teams reduce write-up time by ~70% (from days to hours)
- Conservation authority reporting automated

## Specific Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Publishing the Vehicle Collision Study

**Current Workflow:**
1. Collect collision incident data in field (spreadsheet)
2. Manually compile IUCN status, population estimates
3. Write methods, results, discussion sections from raw data
4. Produce map of collision hotspots

**DataWinder Workflow:**
1. Log incidents in platform (date, location, species, severity)
2. Platform auto-fetches IUCN status for *P. kirkii* (Endangered)
3. AI generates structured methods section (survey design, IUCN compliance framework)
4. Auto-produces hotspot map with conservation priority recommendations
5. Export compliance documentation for conservation authority

**Outcome:** Paper draft ready in days instead of weeks
