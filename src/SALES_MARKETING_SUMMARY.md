## Product Positioning

### The Problem
Conservation researchers waste 60–70% of project time on:
- **Fragmented tools**: Search GBIF, download QGIS, install MaxEnt, write R scripts
- **Manual data cleaning**: Merging datasets, removing duplicates, validating species names
- **Setup overhead**: Java dependencies, GIS config, command-line tools
- **Publication delays**: Re-running analyses when data updates, slow report generation

### The Solution
DataWinder unifies the entire workflow:
- **One platform**: Search, validate, model, report
- **Automatic QA**: AI detects duplicates, outliers, taxonomy mismatches
- **Production-grade**: Publication-ready results in hours
- **No setup**: Cloud-native, zero installation
- **Free for academics**: @bangor.ac.uk = £0 forever

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Multi-Source Integration**: Only platform combining IUCN, GBIF, iNaturalist, SpeciesLink
2. **AI Data Validation**: Automatic duplicate/outlier detection (competitors require manual review)
3. **Climate Scenarios**: Built-in RCP projections (competitors charge extra or require separate tools)
4. **Publication-Ready Reports**: Generate in 1 click (competitors require custom scripts)
5. **Team Collaboration**: Real-time workspaces with version history (competitors are single-user)
6. **Free for Universities**: Removes adoption barriers for academic research

## Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1: Free Academic
- **Target**: University researchers (@bangor.ac.uk, expanding to other universities)
- **Goal**: Build academic user base, generate word-of-mouth, future conversion pipeline
- **Psychology**: Low friction, "good citizen" branding
- **Limits**: 5 projects, 1,000 occurrences/month (enough for most academic work)
- **Upsell Path**: Larger projects → upgrade to Pro

### Tier 2: Trial (14 days)
- **Target**: All non-academic users
- **Goal**: Let users experience full Pro features, reduce conversion risk
- **Psychology**: "Try before buy" reduces hesitation
- **Features**: Full Pro access (no feature gating)
- **Conversion Path**: Day 12 reminder → Day 14 upgrade prompt

### Tier 3: Pro
- **Price**: £99/month or £990/year (2 months free)
- **Target**: Professional researchers, NGOs, government, consultancies
- **Psychology**: Unlimited projects/users removes "growth ceiling"
- **Features**:
  - Ensemble methods (scientific credibility)
  - Climate scenarios (premium deliverable)
  - Photo AI identification (time-saver)
  - API access (automation value)
  - Priority support (peace of mind)

### Enterprise
- **Price**: Custom (based on usage, users, SLA requirements)
- **Target**: Large government agencies, conservation organizations, consultancy chains
- **Features**: Everything Pro + custom integrations, dedicated support engineer, SLA

## Pricing Justification

### Why £99/month?
1. **Competitive Positioning**:
   - QGIS (free) + MaxEnt (free) + R training (~£2,000) = Hidden costs + time
   - DataWinder Pro = £99/month = fully managed alternative
2. **Value Prop**:
   - Saves 10–20 hours/month (vs. manual workflow)
   - At £50/hour (researcher salary), saves £500–1,000/month
   - ROI > 5x in year 1
3. **Market Comparable**:
   - ArcGIS Online: £62/month
   - IBM SPSS: £50/month
   - Datacamp (for data skills): £30/month
4. **Runway**:
   - Need 100 Pro subscribers to reach £9,900/month (cover costs)
   - Achievable in 6 months with current growth rates

### Annual Discount (£990/year vs. £1,188 monthly)
- **Psychology**: "Save 2 months" incentive encourages annual commitment
- **Business**: Improves cash flow (annual billing is 50% of monthly revenue upfront)
- **Conversion**: ~30–40% of Pro users choose annual (industry standard)

## Conversion Funnel & Targets

### Awareness
- **Goal**: 10,000 potential users know about DataWinder
- **Channels**: Content marketing, conferences, partnerships
- **Metric**: 10,000 website visits/month

### Signup
- **Goal**: 5% conversion (500 signups/month)
- **Tactic**: "Start 14-Day Free Trial" CTA on landing page
- **Barrier**: Email verification (prevent spam)

### Activation (Day 1–3)
- **Goal**: 70% of signups create first project and import species
- **Tactic**: Onboarding wizard, email nudges
- **Barrier**: Data import complexity

### Trial (Day 4–14)
- **Goal**: 80% of activated users run at least one model
- **Tactic**: Email reminders (day 3, day 7, day 12)
- **Barrier**: Model interpretation confusion

### Conversion (Day 14+)
- **Goal**: 10–15% of trial users upgrade to Pro
- **Tactic**: Day 12 "3 days left" email, upgrade prompt on day 14
- **Barrier**: Cost/budget decision

### Retention (Day 30+)
- **Goal**: 90% of Pro users retain in month 2
- **Tactic**: Monthly feature updates, success email, support
- **Barrier**: Feature gaps, API availability
