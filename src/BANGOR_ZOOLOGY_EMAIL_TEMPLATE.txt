WHAT IS DATAWINDER?

DataWinder is a comprehensive, integrated platform built on peer-reviewed research frameworks that enables researchers to:

• Discover species from multiple sources: IUCN Red List, iNaturalist observations, GBIF occurrence records, and speciesLink specimens
• Clean and validate occurrence data with outlier detection and quality checks
• Select from multiple climate datasets: WorldClim, CHELSA, ERA5, MODIS, CMIP6 projections
• Run species distribution models using MAXENT automation
• Calculate threat scores integrating habitat loss, population trends, protected area coverage, climate suitability change, and disease risk
• Compare climate scenarios and project future habitat shifts under different warming pathways
• Generate conservation recommendations based on data-driven threat assessment

THE SIX-PHASE RESEARCH PIPELINE:

1. Species Search & Selection
   → Discover species using IUCN, iNaturalist, and GBIF databases

2. Data Tidying & Quality Control
   → Clean, validate, and prepare occurrence data for modeling

3. Climate Data Integration
   → Select climate datasets and variables for your analysis

4. MAXENT Modeling
   → Run species distribution models with performance validation

5. Threat Assessment
   → Evaluate conservation risks and priority rankings

6. Scenario & Ensemble Analysis
   → Compare climate scenarios and project future habitat shifts
