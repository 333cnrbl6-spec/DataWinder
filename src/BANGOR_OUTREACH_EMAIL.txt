We are developing DataWinder — a pioneering biodiversity research platform designed to transform how ecologists, zoologists, and conservation scientists access and analyze multi-source species data.

**What is DataWinder?**

DataWinder integrates species occurrence data from IUCN Red List, iNaturalist, GBIF, and SpeciesLink into a unified research workspace. It streamlines data preparation, enables species distribution modelling using MAXENT, and provides climate projection analysis — all in one intelligent platform.
