DataWinder is now live — a biodiversity research platform that transforms how conservation scientists access and analyze multi-source species data.

**What is DataWinder?**

DataWinder integrates species occurrence data from IUCN Red List, iNaturalist, GBIF, and SpeciesLink into one unified workspace. Run production-grade species distribution models, validate data with AI, project climate futures, and generate publication-ready conservation reports — no complex software setup required.
